The rival king

Pedubast I was a cousin of Takelot II who challenged him for the throne of Thebes. Backed by factions in the north, he sparked a brutal, decades-long civil war for control of Upper Egypt. He repeatedly captured and lost Thebes during his violent struggle against Takelot II and his son, Prince Osorkon.
