Old Kingdom

Userkaf

5th Dynasty · 2494-2345 BCE

Reign begins2494 BCE
Reign ends2487 BCE
Dynasty span2494-2345 BCE

Background

Userkaf founded Dynasty 5 and is especially associated with the growing prominence of the solar cult. He built a pyramid at Saqqara and a sun temple at Abu Ghurab, a new form of royal monument dedicated to Ra. His reign marks a shift in the balance of royal building from the enormous stone pyramids of early Dynasty 4 toward solar temples and smaller pyramids. The foundation of a new dynasty is conventional, but its exact family connection to the preceding house remains debated. His monuments nonetheless document an important reorientation of royal ideology. Research: Metropolitan Museum; Old Kingdom studies.

Chronology: Shaw, Oxford History of Ancient Egypt (2000)

Full profile

The prophecy of the triplets

Userkaf was the founder of the Fifth Dynasty. According to the Middle Kingdom literary tale known as the Westcar Papyrus, a prophecy was given to Pharaoh Khufu predicting that his royal line would be overthrown by three brothers—triplets born to the wife of a high priest of Ra. The story claims these triplets became the first three kings of the Fifth Dynasty, cementing the new dynasty's close relationship with the sun god.

The first sun temple

To legitimize his rule, Userkaf associated himself with past greatness by building his pyramid at Saqqara, as close as possible to Djoser's Step Pyramid. However, his most significant architectural legacy was constructing the very first Sun Temple at Abu Gurob. This initiated a major religious shift, elevating the cult of Ra to unprecedented state importance.
