Full profile

A prosperous era

Amasis seized the throne following the rebellion against Apries, securing his legitimacy by burying his predecessor with full royal honors and marrying his daughter. His long reign was marked by immense economic prosperity and flourishing international trade, particularly with the Greek city-states. He forged close diplomatic ties abroad, sending lavish gifts to rebuild sanctuaries like Delphi, while keeping a watchful eye on the rising threat of the Persian Empire under Cyrus the Great.
