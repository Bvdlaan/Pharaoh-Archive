Middle Kingdom

Amenemhat I (Sehetepibre)

12th Dynasty · 1985-1773 BCE

Reign begins1985 BCE
Reign ends1956 BCE
Dynasty span1985-1773 BCE
Full profile

The usurper and visionary

Amenemhat I founded the 12th Dynasty. He was highly likely the powerful vizier who overthrew the final king of the 11th Dynasty. To consolidate his power and distance himself from the old Theban aristocracy, he relocated the capital from Thebes to a new, strategically located city in the north called Itj-tawy.

A bloody end and a literary legacy

He initiated the practice of co-regency. Crowning his son Senusret I ensured a smooth succession, which proved to be a vital decision. While his son was away on a military campaign in Libya, Amenemhat I was assassinated in a palace coup. His death inspired famous pieces of Middle Kingdom literature. The most notable is the *Instruction of Amenemhat*, a text presented as the murdered king's ghost warning his son not to trust anyone.
