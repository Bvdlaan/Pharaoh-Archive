Osorkon II (Usermaatre)

Reign begins874 BCE
Reign ends850 BCE

Full profile

The Jubilee King

Osorkon II ruled for anywhere from 20 to 40 years. He was fiercely devoted to his ancestral city of Bubastis and the cat goddess Bastet. To celebrate his Jubilee (Sed festival), he constructed a magnificent temple hall specifically for the occasion.

A tolerant pharaoh

Despite his grand building projects in the north, his influence over the south completely evaporated. When his cousin Harsiese declared himself an independent king in Thebes, Osorkon II took no military action against him. This passive tolerance ultimately laid the groundwork for the permanent political fracturing of Egypt.
