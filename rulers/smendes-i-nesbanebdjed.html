Smendes I (Nesbanebdjed)

21st Dynasty (Tanite) · 1069-945 BCE

Reign begins1069 BCE
Reign ends1043 BCE
Dynasty span1069-945 BCE
Full profile

A divided kingdom

Smendes I officially founded the 21st Dynasty following the death of Ramesses XI. He was likely a military man who legitimized his claim to the throne by marrying Tentamun, a daughter of Ramesses IX. He moved the official capital to the northern city of Tanis because the Nile branch near the former capital of Pi-Ramesses had silted up.

The agreement with Thebes

In reality, Smendes only controlled Lower Egypt (from the Mediterranean to just south of the Fayum). Upper Egypt was entirely under the control of Herihor and the High Priests of Amun in Thebes. Because Smendes lacked the manpower to conquer the south, and the priests did not wish to march north, the two factions reached an unofficial agreement to peacefully coexist while acknowledging the god Amun as the ultimate ruler of a divided Egypt.

The report of Wenamun

Smendes is prominently featured in the ancient literary text known as the *Report of Wenamun*. The text describes an Egyptian priest traveling to Byblos to purchase cedar wood, only to be robbed and treated with intense disrespect by the local Canaanite ruler. Historians agree this story perfectly reflects the severe collapse of Egyptian prestige and imperial authority abroad during Smendes's reign.
