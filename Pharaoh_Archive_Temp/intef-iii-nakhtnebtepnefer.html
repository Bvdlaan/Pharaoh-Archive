Intef III (Nakhtnebtepnefer)

Reign dates: 2063-2055 BCE

Dynasty span: 11th Dynasty (Theban, early)

## Holding the line Intef III had a relatively brief reign of around eight years. Rather than launching massive new offensives, his primary achievement was maintaining and heavily defending the territorial gains made by his father, Intef II. ## Defending Abydos He successfully repelled a major counter-attack from the northern Herakleopolitan forces. By holding the line at Abydos, he secured the necessary strategic advantage that allowed his son to finally end the civil war and reunite Egypt.
