Menkaure (Mycerinus)

Reign dates: 2532-2503 BCE

Dynasty span: 4th Dynasty

## Economic decline Menkaure succeeded his father Khafre around 2530 BCE. Later texts remembered him as a benevolent and generous king [00:13:16]. However, the astronomical costs and decades of manpower required by his predecessors' monuments appear to have depleted the royal treasury [00:13:46]. Because resources were in short supply, Menkaure's pyramid at Giza is only a fraction of the size of Khufu's and Khafre's, standing 65 meters high [00:14:12]. Next to his pyramid sit three smaller structures built for his wives [00:14:29]. ## A legacy in stone While his pyramid was smaller, Menkaure surpassed his ancestors in the sheer volume of highly realistic sculptures and portraits commissioned during his reign [00:14:38]. These stunning slate statues often depict the king flanked by deities, showcasing the peak of Old Kingdom artistry [00:14:46]. His original crown prince, Khuenre, died prematurely, leaving the throne to Shepseskaf, a son from a different wife [00:15:06].
