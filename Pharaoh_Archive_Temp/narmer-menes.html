Narmer (Menes?)

Reign dates: c.3000 BCE

Dynasty span: 1st Dynasty

## The unifier of two lands Narmer is widely recognized as the king who unified Upper and Lower Egypt, establishing the First Dynasty. The famous Narmer Palette provides crucial early evidence of this, depicting him wearing the White Crown of Upper Egypt on one side and the Red Crown of Lower Egypt on the other. He is heavily associated with the legendary king Menes, whom ancient chroniclers credit with founding the capital city of Memphis to control the newly unified state.
