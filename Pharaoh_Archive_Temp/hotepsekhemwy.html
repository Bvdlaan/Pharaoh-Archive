Hotepsekhemwy

Reign dates: c.2890 BCE

Dynasty span: 2nd Dynasty

## The transition of power Hotepsekhemwy was the first king of the Second Dynasty. The transition of power from the First Dynasty does not appear to have been violent. Evidence shows that he actually presided over the funeral of Qa'a (the final king of the First Dynasty), as Hotepsekhemwy's seals were discovered at the entrance to Qa'a's royal tomb in Abydos. ## Breaking burial traditions He broke with the established tradition of being buried in the royal cemetery of Abydos. Instead, Hotepsekhemwy chose the great cemetery at Saqqara, near the capital city of Memphis, as his final resting place. ## A debated timeline Despite his name being found on many artifacts, extremely little is known about his reign. The Turin Canon claims he ruled for an impossible 95 years, which historians reject. The ancient historian Manetho recorded a 38-year reign and noted that a disastrous, deadly earthquake occurred during his rule.
