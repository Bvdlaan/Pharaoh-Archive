Userkaf

Reign dates: 2494-2487 BCE

Dynasty span: 5th Dynasty

## The prophecy of the triplets Userkaf was the founder of the Fifth Dynasty. According to the Middle Kingdom literary tale known as the Westcar Papyrus, a prophecy was given to Pharaoh Khufu predicting that his royal line would be overthrown by three brothers—triplets born to the wife of a high priest of Ra. The story claims these triplets became the first three kings of the Fifth Dynasty, cementing the new dynasty's close relationship with the sun god. ## The first sun temple To legitimize his rule, Userkaf associated himself with past greatness by building his pyramid at Saqqara, as close as possible to Djoser's Step Pyramid. However, his most significant architectural legacy was constructing the very first Sun Temple at Abu Gurob. This initiated a major religious shift, elevating the cult of Ra to unprecedented state importance.
