Amenemhat II (Nubkaure)

Reign dates: 1911-1877 BCE

Dynasty span: 12th Dynasty

## An era of diplomacy Amenemhat II enjoyed a long, highly prosperous, and peaceful reign. Instead of focusing on military conquest, he prioritized international trade and diplomacy. Archaeological discoveries from his era reveal massive caches of foreign silver, lapis lazuli, and Minoan artifacts. The famous Tod Treasure highlights Egypt's vast commercial reach into the Levant and the Aegean during his rule.
