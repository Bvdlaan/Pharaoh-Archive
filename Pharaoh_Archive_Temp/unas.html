Unas

Reign dates: 2375-2345 BCE

Dynasty span: 5th Dynasty

## The Pyramid Texts Unas was the final king of the Fifth Dynasty. While his pyramid at Saqqara was relatively small and unassuming on the outside, its interior revolutionized Egyptian funerary practices. Unas was the very first pharaoh to have the walls of his burial chamber entirely covered in carved, blue-painted hieroglyphs. ## Guaranteeing the afterlife These inscriptions, known today as the Pyramid Texts, are the oldest known religious writings in the world. They consist of hundreds of magical spells and incantations designed to protect the pharaoh's soul and guarantee his safe passage into the afterlife, reflecting the rising theological power of Osiris, the god of the underworld.
