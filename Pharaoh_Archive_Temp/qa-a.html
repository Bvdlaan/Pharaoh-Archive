Qa'a

Reign dates: c.2906-2890 BCE

Dynasty span: 1st Dynasty

## The final king of the first dynasty Qa'a restored order and ruled for about 33 years. He built the last massive tomb of the First Dynasty at Abydos, which included dozens of subsidiary burials. His death marks the definitive end of the royal line. The transition to the Second Dynasty appears peaceful, as his successor Hotepsekhemwy oversaw his funeral and sealed his tomb.
