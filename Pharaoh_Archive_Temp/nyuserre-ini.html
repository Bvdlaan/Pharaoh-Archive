Nyuserre Ini

Reign dates: 2445-2421 BCE

Dynasty span: 5th Dynasty

## The great builder of Abusir Nyuserre enjoyed a long and stable reign, allowing him to heavily dominate the landscape of Abusir. Because both Neferefre and Neferirkare died before their monuments were finished, Nyuserre took on the responsibility of completing their complexes alongside building his own. ## The peak of the sun cult He constructed the largest and most complete Sun Temple of the entire dynasty at Abu Gurob. The remains of this temple feature a massive stone obelisk and highly detailed reliefs depicting the cycles of nature and the seasons, celebrating the life-giving power of the sun god Ra.
