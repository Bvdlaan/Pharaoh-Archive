Nitiqret (Nitocris)

Reign dates: 2184-2181 BCE

Dynasty span: 6th Dynasty

## The legendary queen Nitiqret (often known by the Greek name Nitocris) is an incredibly controversial historical figure. According to Manetho, she was the final ruler of the Sixth Dynasty, describing her as "braver than all the men of her time" and the most beautiful woman in the world. ## A historical fiction? Despite these legendary descriptions, there is absolutely no contemporary archaeological evidence to prove she existed. Furthermore, modern linguistic analysis suggests the name "Nitiqret" is actually a masculine name. Whether Nitiqret was a powerful queen, a male ruler, or an entirely fabricated legend, the end of this reign marked the definitive collapse of the monarchy. Egypt dissolved into civil war, officially ending the Old Kingdom period.
