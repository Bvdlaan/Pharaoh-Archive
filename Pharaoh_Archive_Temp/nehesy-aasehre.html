Nehesy (Aasehre)

Reign dates: early–middle 14th Dynasty; exact dates disputed

Dynasty span: 14th Dynasty

## The best-attested king Nehesy is the most well-documented ruler of the highly obscure 14th Dynasty. Historians believe he was the first, second, or sixth king of the sequence. His parentage reflects the diverse demographics of the Nile Delta at the time. His father was likely of Canaanite ancestry, and his mother was Nubian.
