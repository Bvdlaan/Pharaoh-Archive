Den (Dewen)

Reign dates: c.2945 BCE

Dynasty span: 1st Dynasty

## The double crown Den enjoyed a remarkably long reign, ruling for roughly 50 years. He was a major innovator in royal tradition. He was the first pharaoh to officially use the "King of Upper and Lower Egypt" title. He is also the earliest king depicted wearing the combined Double Crown, symbolizing total and absolute authority over the entire country. ## Architectural advancements His tomb at Abydos introduced significant architectural advancements. It featured a grand staircase, allowing the tomb to be sealed after the burial, and a floor made of red granite imported from Aswan. He also held the first recorded Sed festival to rejuvenate his royal power.
