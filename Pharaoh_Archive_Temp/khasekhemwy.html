Khasekhemwy

Reign dates: c.2720-2686 BCE

Dynasty span: 2nd Dynasty

## The Peacemaker of the Second Dynasty Khasekhemwy is regarded as the final king of the Second Dynasty and the great unifier of Egypt. He brought internal peace and stability to a fractured country, which laid the crucial groundwork for the monumental architectural achievements of the upcoming Old Kingdom. ## The two powers are at peace He began his reign as *Khasekhem* ("The power has appeared") using a traditional Horus serekh. After violently subduing a rebellion in Lower Egypt, he sought to unify the fractured religious and political factions. He changed his name to *Khasekhemwy* ("The two powers have appeared" or "The two lords are at peace in him") and uniquely placed both the Horus falcon and the Seth animal on top of his royal serekh. ## Military campaigns and a massive tomb He also campaigned externally, battling foreigners in Nubia (known as *Ta-Seti*, the "Land of the Bow"). He ruled for roughly 18 years and died around 2690 BCE. He was buried in Abydos in a staggering complex that was the largest of its time, featuring a 58-room burial structure and a massive fortress-like enclosure known today as *Shunet el-Zebib*.
