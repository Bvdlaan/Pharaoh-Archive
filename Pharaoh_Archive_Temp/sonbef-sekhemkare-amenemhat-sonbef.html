Sonbef (Sekhemkare Amenemhat Sonbef)

Reign dates: early 13th Dynasty; exact dates disputed

Dynasty span: 13th Dynasty

## The true founder For many years, historians identified a king named Wegaf as the founder of the 13th Dynasty. Recent scholarship has revised this consensus. Most experts now agree that Sobekhotep I was the first ruler of the dynasty, while Wegaf ruled much later in the sequence.
