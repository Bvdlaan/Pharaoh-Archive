Weneg

Reign dates: c.2810 BCE

Dynasty span: 2nd Dynasty

## A localized ruler? Weneg's reign is highly contested and shrouded in mystery. His name has only been discovered on artifacts in Saqqara. Because of this lack of widespread evidence, historians suspect that if he did rule, his authority may have been strictly limited to the capital of Memphis, with little to no control over the rest of Egypt. No tomb has ever been found.
