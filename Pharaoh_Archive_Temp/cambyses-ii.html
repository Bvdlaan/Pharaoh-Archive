Cambyses II

Reign dates: 525-522 BCE

Dynasty span: 27th Dynasty (First Persian Period)

The Persian conquest

Cambyses II led the massive Persian invasion of Egypt in 525 BCE, defeating Psamtik III at the decisive Battle of Pelusium. He successfully captured Memphis and assumed the traditional pharaonic title of King of Upper and Lower Egypt. While later Greek and Egyptian accounts by Herodotus portray him as a ruthless and sacrilegious tyrant who mocked Egyptian religion, contemporary archaeological evidence suggests he largely respected local traditions, maintained native administrative structures, and patronized Egyptian temples.
