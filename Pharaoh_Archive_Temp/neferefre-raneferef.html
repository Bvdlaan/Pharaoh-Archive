Neferefre (Raneferef)

Reign dates: 2448-2445 BCE

Dynasty span: 5th Dynasty

## The unfinished monument Neferefre also experienced a very short reign, dying unexpectedly in his early twenties. Due to his sudden death, his pyramid at Abusir was hastily abandoned and converted into a flat, square mastaba so he could be buried without delay. Despite its unfinished state, archaeologists discovered a massive cache of papyri and beautifully preserved statues of the king inside his hastily constructed mortuary temple.
