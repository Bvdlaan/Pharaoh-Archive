Shoshenq III

Reign dates: c. 837–798/785 BCE

Dynasty span: 22nd Dynasty (Bubastite/Libyan)

## Governing a fractured land Shoshenq III ruled in Tanis for nearly 40 years. Rather than engaging directly in the massive civil war consuming the south, he took a largely laissez-faire approach to governing. He implicitly supported rival factions against Takelot II but primarily focused on maintaining control over the Delta region as the political power of the 22nd Dynasty slowly eroded.
