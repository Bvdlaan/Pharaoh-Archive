Necho II (Wehemibre)

Reign dates: 610-595 BCE

Dynasty span: 26th Dynasty (Saite)

Ambition in the Levant

Necho II succeeded his father and aggressively pushed Egyptian influence into the Near East. He led a military campaign into the Levant to assist the remnants of the Assyrian Empire against the rising power of Babylon, clashing with Babylonian forces at Carchemish. Beyond military maneuvers, he was an ambitious builder and organizer, reportedly initiating a canal connecting the Nile to the Red Sea and commissioning a three-year circumnavigation of Africa by Phoenician sailors.
