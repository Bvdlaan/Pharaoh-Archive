Peribsen (Seth-Peribsen)

Reign dates: c.2760 BCE

Dynasty span: 2nd Dynasty

## A radical religious shift Peribsen was a radical figure who briefly broke with ancient Egyptian religious tradition, predating Akhenaten's famous religious rebellion by over 13 centuries. Since the predynastic period, kings used a *serekh* (a royal crest) topped with a falcon representing the god Horus. Peribsen shocked tradition by dropping the Horus falcon entirely, replacing it with the "Seth animal"—the symbol of the god Set (Seth). ## Reunification and burial It is unknown whether he was a usurper or a royal from Upper Egypt who mobilized the followers of Seth. Regardless, by the end of his reign, he successfully reunited Upper and Lower Egypt. He also returned to the ancient burial traditions of the First Dynasty by placing his tomb in the royal cemetery of Abydos. His tomb was relatively small (16 by 13 meters) and utilized a mud-brick burial chamber, unlike the wood-lined tombs of the previous dynasty.
