Raneb (Nebra)

Reign dates: c.2865 BCE

Dynasty span: 2nd Dynasty

## Lord of the Sun There is very scant material evidence surviving from his reign, and historians even debate the exact reading of his name. Depending on the translation of the hieroglyphs, he is known as either Raneb ("Ra is my lord") or Nebra ("Lord of the Sun"). ## Continuing the Saqqara tradition Like his predecessor, Raneb does not appear to have a tomb in the ancestral royal cemetery of Abydos. Seals bearing his name were found inside Hotepsekhemwy's tomb in Saqqara, indicating that Raneb likely oversaw his predecessor's funeral and chose to be buried in Saqqara as well.
