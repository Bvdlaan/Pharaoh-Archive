Djedefre (Radjedef)

Reign dates: 2566-2558 BCE

Dynasty span: 4th Dynasty

## A shift in succession Djedefre succeeded his father Khufu in 2566 BCE and oversaw his burial [00:09:34]. He was not the original crown prince; the kingship was meant to pass to his brother Kawab, but Kawab died shortly before Khufu [00:09:44]. ## The unfinished pyramid During his brief eight-year reign, Djedefre attempted to follow his father's legacy by building an even larger pyramid [00:09:59]. He moved the royal construction site to Abu Rawash. However, because his reign was so short, the monument was never completed [00:10:07]. The succeeding king directed resources away from Abu Rawash to build his own funerary complex [00:10:16].
