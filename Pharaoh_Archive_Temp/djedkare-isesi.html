Djedkare Isesi

Reign dates: 2414-2375 BCE

Dynasty span: 5th Dynasty

## A shift in religious power Djedkare Isesi ruled for nearly four decades. His reign marked a definitive end to the construction of solar temples, signaling a deliberate shift in state religion. The absolute dominance of the Ra priesthood was curtailed, and the cult of Osiris began to rise in prominence among the general population. He moved the royal burial grounds even further south into South Saqqara. ## The Maxims of Ptahhotep His reign is also famous for his vizier, Ptahhotep. Ptahhotep authored *The Maxims of Ptahhotep*, a foundational piece of ancient Egyptian "wisdom literature" that offered philosophical instructions on morality, humility, and proper societal behavior.
