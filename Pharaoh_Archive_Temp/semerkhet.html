Semerkhet

Reign dates: c.2915 BCE

Dynasty span: 1st Dynasty

## The usurper Semerkhet likely took the throne by force. He deliberately erased Anedjib's name from numerous stone vessels and monuments, a classic sign of usurpation and political hostility. His reign was brief, lasting around eight and a half years. The ancient historian Manetho later recorded that his rule was plagued by numerous disasters.
