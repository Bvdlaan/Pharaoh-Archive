Ramesses VIII (Usermaatre Akhenamun)

Reign dates: 1129-1126 BCE

Dynasty span: 20th Dynasty

## An ephemeral ruler Ramesses VIII, another son of Ramesses III, took the throne as an elderly man. He ruled for less than a year. His incredibly short reign reflects the rapid turnover and instability of the late 20th Dynasty.
