Userkare

Reign dates: 2323-2321 BCE

Dynasty span: 6th Dynasty

## The phantom king Userkare is a highly enigmatic figure. His name appears on the Abydos King List, yet there are virtually no contemporary archaeological records to prove his existence. Because of this lack of evidence, many scholars suspect he was a usurper. If the rumors of Teti's assassination are true, Userkare may have been a conspirator who seized the throne for a year or less before Teti's true heir, Pepi I, could secure power.
