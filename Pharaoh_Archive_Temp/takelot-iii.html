Takelot III

Reign dates: c. 767-755 BCE

Dynasty span: 23rd Dynasty (Libyan)

## A fading dynasty Takelot III succeeded Osorkon III. His reign is poorly documented in the archaeological record, representing the rapid decline of the 23rd Dynasty. The royal family in Thebes became increasingly divided by internal sibling and clan rivalries.
