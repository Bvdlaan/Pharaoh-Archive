Neferirkare Kakai

Reign dates: 2475-2455 BCE

Dynasty span: 5th Dynasty

## The dual royal names Neferirkare was the first Egyptian pharaoh known to officially separate his royal titles into two distinct cartouches: a prenomen (throne name) and a nomen (birth name), a tradition that all subsequent pharaohs would follow. ## The Abusir Papyri His pyramid complex at Abusir yielded one of the most important administrative discoveries in Egyptology: the Abusir Papyri. These extensive temple archives provide historians with an incredibly detailed, day-to-day look at the accounting, duty rosters, and religious administration of the Old Kingdom priesthood.
