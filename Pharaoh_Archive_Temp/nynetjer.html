Nynetjer

Reign dates: c.2840 BCE

Dynasty span: 2nd Dynasty

## A well-documented reign Nynetjer ruled for approximately 35 years. His reign is one of the best-documented of the era thanks to the Palermo Stone. The records indicate he presided over numerous religious festivals, ordered multiple country-wide censuses, oversaw the running of the Apis Bull, and conducted processional tours for the god Horus. ## Civil unrest in the North Despite his long reign, almost all of his recorded activities are centered around Memphis. However, a specific register on the Palermo Stone mentions "attacking Shemra and Mehu." Since "Mehu" translates to "House of the North" (referring to the Nile Delta region of Lower Egypt), many scholars believe Nynetjer faced a civil war or rebellion in the northern territories. He has no known tomb in Abydos.
