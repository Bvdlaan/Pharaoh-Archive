Shepseskaf

Reign dates: 2503-2498 BCE

Dynasty span: 4th Dynasty

## A break from Giza Shepseskaf became king around 2500 BCE and ruled for only four years, making his reign the shortest of the Fourth Dynasty [00:15:15]. He notably broke with the tradition of his immediate ancestors by choosing not to be buried at Giza [00:15:33]. Instead of a pyramid, which he either chose not to build or could no longer afford, his final resting place was a large mastaba at Saqqara [00:15:43]. ## The end of the dynasty His reign may have been marked by internal friction between the royal family and the increasingly powerful priesthood of Ra [00:15:33]. The transition following his death is murky, heavily involving a highly important royal woman named Khentkaus [00:15:59]. While scholars debate her exact relationship to Shepseskaf—whether she was his mother, his wife, or a regent—ancient king lists universally agree that Shepseskaf was the final ruler of the Fourth Dynasty [00:16:16].
