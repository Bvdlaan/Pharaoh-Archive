Intef II (Wahankh)

Reign dates: 2112-2063 BCE

Dynasty span: 11th Dynasty (Theban, early)

## The 50-year war Intef II ruled for nearly 50 years and spent almost his entire reign engaged in relentless warfare against the Herakleopolitan kings. He successfully pushed the Theban borders northward, capturing the highly strategic and sacred city of Abydos. This victory cut off the northern kings from the resources of Upper Egypt. ## The stela of the dogs Aside from his military conquests, Intef II left a unique personal legacy. A famous stela from his tomb depicts him with his beloved hunting dogs, even recording their foreign names. He was also the first ruler to build significant monuments to the god Amun at Karnak, elevating the local deity to national prominence.
