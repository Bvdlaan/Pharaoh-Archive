Osorkon IV

Reign dates: c. 732/730–722 BCE

Dynasty span: 23rd Dynasty (classification disputed)

## The last king of Tanis Osorkon IV was the final ruler of the 22nd Dynasty. His power barely extended beyond the immediate vicinity of Tanis. As his reign concluded, Egypt faced massive external threats: the Kushite armies advancing rapidly from the south and the Neo-Assyrian Empire expanding relentlessly from the east.
