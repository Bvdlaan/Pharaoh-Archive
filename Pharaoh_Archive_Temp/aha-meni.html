Aha (Meni)

Reign dates: c.2990 BCE

Dynasty span: 1st Dynasty

## Consolidating the kingdom Aha succeeded Narmer and focused on securing the fragile new state. He established a firm administrative grip over the Nile Delta and launched military campaigns against neighboring Nubia. ## The practice of retainer sacrifice His burial complex in the royal cemetery of Abydos provides some of the earliest clear evidence of retainer sacrifice. Dozens of servants and officials were killed and buried alongside him, intended to serve the king in the afterlife. This brutal practice was characteristic of the First Dynasty but was eventually phased out in later eras.
