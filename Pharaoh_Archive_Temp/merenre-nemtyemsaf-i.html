Merenre Nemtyemsaf I

Reign dates: 2287-2278 BCE

Dynasty span: 6th Dynasty

## Expanding into Africa Merenre ruled for a brief period, likely no more than five or six years. His most significant achievement was expanding Egypt's reach deeper into the African continent. Through his official Harkhuf, Merenre sent numerous trading expeditions into Nubia and the mysterious "Land of Yam" (likely located in modern-day northern Sudan). ## Military alliances When the Land of Yam was attacked by its enemies, Harkhuf and Egyptian soldiers fought alongside them, securing a powerful alliance. To solidify these ties, Merenre personally traveled to Upper Egypt to hold a summit with representatives from the major Nubian tribes, ensuring the continuous flow of precious woods and ivory into Egypt.
