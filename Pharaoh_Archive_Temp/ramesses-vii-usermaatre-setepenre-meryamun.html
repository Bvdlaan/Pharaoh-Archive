Ramesses VII (Usermaatre Setepenre Meryamun)

Reign dates: 1136-1129 BCE

Dynasty span: 20th Dynasty

## Continued decline Ramesses VII ruled for approximately seven years. His reign saw continued economic turmoil, soaring grain prices, and rampant inflation. The prestige of the royal household plummeted as the country drifted further into poverty.
