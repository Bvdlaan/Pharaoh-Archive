Shepseskare

Reign dates: 2455-2448 BCE

Dynasty span: 5th Dynasty

## An ephemeral ruler Shepseskare is one of the most obscure pharaohs of the Old Kingdom. His reign was exceptionally brief, likely lasting no more than a few months or a year. Material evidence from his time on the throne is nearly nonexistent, save for a few clay seals and the foundations of an unfinished pyramid located in the northernmost part of Abusir.
