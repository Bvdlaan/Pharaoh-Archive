Anedjib

Reign dates: c.2925 BCE

Dynasty span: 1st Dynasty

## A troubled succession Anedjib succeeded Den, but his reign was short and fraught with political difficulties. He held an early Sed festival, a ritual usually reserved for a pharaoh's 30th year on the throne, possibly to reassert his weakening authority. His tomb at Abydos is unusually small and poorly constructed compared to his predecessor, reflecting a sudden decline in royal resources or political stability.
