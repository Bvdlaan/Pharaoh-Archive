Darius I

Reign dates: 522/521-486/485 BCE

Dynasty span: 27th Dynasty (First Persian Period)

Administrative integration and codification

Darius I ruled Egypt as part of the vast Achaemenid Empire while implementing policies that respected and supported local religious institutions. He famously ordered the codification of Egyptian laws in consultation with native priests. He also focused heavily on infrastructure, completing the Red Sea canal project initiated by earlier Saite pharaohs, which connected the Nile to the Red Sea and boosted international trade across the empire.
