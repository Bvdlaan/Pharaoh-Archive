Merenre Nemtyemsaf II

Reign dates: 2184 BCE

Dynasty span: 6th Dynasty

## A fractured succession Merenre II succeeded his elderly father, Pepi II, but his reign was incredibly short. By this point, the centralized authority of the pharaoh had completely broken down, and it is highly unlikely that he was officially recognized as the king over the entirety of a fractured Egypt.
