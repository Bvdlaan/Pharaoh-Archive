Djer (Iti)

Reign dates: c.2970 BCE

Dynasty span: 1st Dynasty

## A long and prosperous rule Djer ruled for decades, bringing immense wealth and stability to the early Egyptian state. He expanded trade networks into the Levant and conducted military expeditions to secure the desert borders. ## The tomb of Osiris His massive tomb at Abydos was so impressive that later generations of Egyptians forgot it belonged to him. By the Middle Kingdom, the site was widely revered as the mythological tomb of the god Osiris. It became a major pilgrimage destination for ancient Egyptians seeking blessings in the afterlife.
