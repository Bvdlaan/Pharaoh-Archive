Khaba

Reign dates: 2640-2637 BCE

Dynasty span: 3rd Dynasty

## The Layer Pyramid Historical records offer almost no details about the events of Khaba's reign. The Turin Canon credits him with a brief seven-year rule. His primary legacy is the construction of the "Layer Pyramid" located at Zawiyet el-Aryan, a few miles south of modern Cairo. Similar to his predecessor Sekhemkhet, the pyramid appears to have never been used for a burial. The location of the king's physical remains is unknown.
