Merneptah (Baenre)

Reign dates: 1213-1203 BCE

Dynasty span: 19th Dynasty

## The 13th son Merneptah was the 13th son of Ramesses II. Because his father lived to be around 90 years old, Merneptah outlived his 12 older brothers and took the throne as an elderly man. ## Defending the borders and the Israel stele His reign required immediate military action to defend Egypt's borders. He successfully crushed a massive invasion by Libyan forces under King Meryey in the western delta. To commemorate this victory, he erected a monument known today as the Merneptah Stele. The final lines of this text briefly document his military campaigns in Canaan and contain the earliest known historical reference to a people called Israel.
