Queen Merneith (regent)

Reign dates: c.2950 BCE

Dynasty span: 1st Dynasty

## The first female ruler When Djet died, his heir Den was too young to rule. Queen Merneith stepped in as regent, making her the first recorded female ruler in ancient Egyptian history. ## Royal burial honors She governed with full royal authority. She is the only woman from the Early Dynastic Period to receive a massive, king-sized tomb in the royal cemetery of Abydos, complete with her own subsidiary burials and stone stelae. This confirms she held unprecedented political power and respect.
