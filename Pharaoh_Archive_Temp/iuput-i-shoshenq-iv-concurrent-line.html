Iuput I / Shoshenq IV (concurrent line)

Reign dates: c. 816–790 BCE (overlapping)

Dynasty span: 23rd Dynasty (Libyan)

## The co-ruler in Thebes Iuput I was the son of Pedubast I. When his father successfully seized Thebes for a second time, he elevated Iuput I to the position of co-ruler. The timeline of his exact reign is heavily disputed, but his faction was ultimately defeated and expelled from Thebes by Prince Osorkon.
