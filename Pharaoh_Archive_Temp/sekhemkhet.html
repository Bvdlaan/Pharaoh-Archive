Sekhemkhet

Reign dates: 2648-2640 BCE

Dynasty span: 3rd Dynasty

## The buried pyramid Sekhemkhet remained an obscure historical figure until 1951, when an unfinished step pyramid was discovered buried deep in the sands of Saqqara. Architectural plans suggest the structure was intended to surpass Djoser's monument, featuring seven steps rather than six. The project was likely abandoned due to Sekhemkhet's short rule. Historical records indicate he reigned for only six or seven years. ## The empty sarcophagus Beneath the unfinished pyramid, archaeologists located Sekhemkhet's alabaster sarcophagus. It was found completely intact, sealed tight with its original gypsum mortar. When researchers opened it in 1954, they discovered it was entirely empty. The actual resting place of the king's body remains an unsolved mystery. Other remaining evidence of his rule includes a rock inscription in the Sinai depicting him smiting a captive, and a seal impression found as far south as Elephantine island.
