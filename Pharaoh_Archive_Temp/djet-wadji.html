Djet (Wadji)

Reign dates: c.2955 BCE

Dynasty span: 1st Dynasty

## The serpent king Djet is famous for the stunning artistry of his reign. His royal monument, the Serpent Stela, is considered a masterpiece of Early Dynastic art. It prominently features the Horus falcon perched on a serekh containing a snake, clearly demonstrating the high level of skill achieved by Egyptian stone carvers early in the kingdom's history. His reign appears to have been relatively short.
