Ramesses XI (Menmaatre Setepenptah)

Reign dates: 1099-1069 BCE

Dynasty span: 20th Dynasty

## The collapse of the New Kingdom Ramesses XI was the final pharaoh of the New Kingdom. During his reign, the central government entirely lost control of Upper Egypt. Desperate citizens in Thebes violently ousted the corrupt High Priest of Amun, Amenhotep, leading to a civil war known as the "War of the High Priest." ## A fractured state The conflict ended when a general named Herihor took military control of Thebes. Herihor declared himself both vizier and High Priest of Amun, effectively operating as an independent king in the south and initiating what he called the "Era of the Renaissance." When Ramesses XI died without a male heir, a military commander named Smendes assumed power in the north, moving the capital to Tanis. This official split marked the end of the New Kingdom and plunged Egypt into the Third Intermediate Period.
