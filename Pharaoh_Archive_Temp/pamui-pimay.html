Pamui (Pimay)

Reign dates: c. 785–774 BCE

Dynasty span: 22nd Dynasty (Bubastite/Libyan)

## An obscure reign Pamui ruled during the late stages of the 22nd Dynasty. Information regarding his reign is incredibly scarce, reflecting the continued decline in royal authority and monument construction in the north.
