Sahure

Reign dates: 2487-2475 BCE

Dynasty span: 5th Dynasty

## Naval fleets and expansion Sahure succeeded Userkaf and moved the royal burial ground to Abusir, a site that would become the hallmark of the Fifth Dynasty. His reign was marked by significant foreign trade and military expansion. The elaborately carved reliefs in his mortuary complex vividly illustrate his massive naval fleet. These inscriptions detail maritime expeditions to the Levantine coast for cedar wood, trading missions to the mysterious land of Punt, and military campaigns against Libyan tribes in the western desert.
