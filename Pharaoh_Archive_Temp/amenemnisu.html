Amenemnisu

Reign dates: 1043-1039 BCE

Dynasty span: 21st Dynasty (Tanite)

## An obscure successor Amenemnisu succeeded Smendes I and ruled for approximately four years. Almost nothing is known about his life or his exact relationship to his predecessor; he may have been a son, a brother, or another relative. His existence was not even confirmed by archaeologists until 1940.
