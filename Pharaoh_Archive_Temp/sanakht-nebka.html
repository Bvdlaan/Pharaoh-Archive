Sanakht (Nebka)

Reign dates: 2686-2667 BCE

Dynasty span: 3rd Dynasty

## The debate over succession Ancient king lists, including the Abydos King List and the Turin Canon, record Sanakht (also known as Nebka) as the first sovereign of the Third Dynasty. Modern archaeological evidence strongly disputes this sequence. Physical evidence demonstrates that Djoser presided over the burial of the final Second Dynasty king Khasekhemwy, indicating Djoser was the direct successor. As a result, modern historians place Sanakht later in the dynasty's chronology.
