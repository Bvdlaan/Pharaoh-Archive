Senusret III (Khakaure)

Reign dates: 1870-1831 BCE

Dynasty span: 12th Dynasty

## The warrior king Senusret III is considered the most powerful and ruthless pharaoh of the Middle Kingdom. He led devastating military campaigns into Nubia, pushing the Egyptian border all the way to the Third Cataract. He constructed a massive, interconnected chain of mud-brick fortresses along the Nile, such as those at Buhen and Semna, to maintain absolute military control. ## Breaking the nomarchs Internally, he completely shattered the power of the provincial governors. These nomarchs had operated like independent warlords since the First Intermediate Period. Senusret III centralized all authority back to the royal court in Itj-tawy. ## The burden of power His royal statuary represents a radical departure from traditional Egyptian idealism. Rather than appearing as a young, divine god, statues of Senusret III portray him with deep wrinkles, heavy eyelids, and a melancholic expression. This artistic choice suggests the heavy, exhausting burden of ruling an empire.
