Osorkon the Elder (Osochor)

Reign dates: 984-978 BCE

Dynasty span: 21st Dynasty (Tanite)

## The first Libyan pharaoh Osorkon the Elder remains a highly mysterious figure whose existence was only confirmed in 1963. He was the son of Shoshenq, a great chief of the Meshwesh (a powerful Libyan tribe). He ruled for eight years. His ascension marks a significant cultural shift: a member of a once-feared Libyan tribe—the sworn enemies of the New Kingdom pharaohs—was now recognized as the legitimate pharaoh of Egypt.
