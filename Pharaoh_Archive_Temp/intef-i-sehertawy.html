Intef I (Sehertawy)

Reign dates: 2125-2112 BCE

Dynasty span: 11th Dynasty (Theban, early)

## Challenging the north Intef I was the first member of the Theban ruling family to explicitly claim the title of pharaoh. By adopting a Horus name—Sehertawy, meaning "He who brings peace to the two lands"—he directly challenged the authority of the 10th Dynasty kings ruling from Herakleopolis in the north. ## A localized king Despite his grand titles, his actual authority was strictly limited to the southern regions around Thebes. His reign marked the official beginning of a brutal, multi-generational civil war between the northern Herakleopolitan state and the rising Theban dynasty.
