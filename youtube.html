The archive is not only something to read — it is becoming something to watch. Pharaoh Archive now has its own YouTube channel, where the same research turns into short, clear videos about the pharaohs and the world they ruled.

Short videos, grounded in research

We are making short videos on individual pharaohs and the dynasties they belonged to — across the Old, Middle and New Kingdoms, and through the turbulent Intermediate Periods in between. Each one is built on careful, well-sourced research. The aim is the same as this website: to make history that is actually worth knowing clear, accurate and easy to follow, rather than to churn out filler.

Made for learning and teaching

The channel is built for the same people this archive is: students who want to learn, teachers who want material they can trust, hobbyists and lifelong enthusiasts, and anyone simply curious about Ancient Egypt. Watch an episode to get your bearings, then dive into the full chronology and the encyclopedia entries here on the site. We hope it inspires people to learn — and helps others teach.

Like, subscribe, and help it grow

If you enjoy the videos, the most helpful thing you can do is like and subscribe. It sounds small, but it is exactly what helps a young channel grow — reaching more classrooms and more curious minds, keeping this website and its mission moving forward: teaching people, and sharing good, reliable information about Ancient Egypt. Every subscriber really does help.
