About the archive

Where curiosity meets AI

← Back to the chronology

This website began with a simple frustration: there was no properly designed, single place to see all of ancient Egypt's dynasties and rulers at a glance. What did exist was scattered, dense, or hard to read. So we built the resource we wished already existed.

Science, made with AI

At heart, the Pharaoh Archive is a piece of scientific research carried out with the help of AI. It is a demonstration of what artificial intelligence can do when the aim is to publish genuinely interesting scientific material — gathering it, structuring it, and presenting it in a way that is clear, accurate, and pleasant to explore.

Passion, design, and technology

It is also a showcase of something we care about deeply: that you can combine a personal passion or hobby with thoughtful design and AI, and end up with something both rigorous and beautiful. That blend of curiosity, craft, and technology is exactly what our company is about.

An open invitation

We would love for you to build on what we have started. Whether you are a student, a hobbyist, a teacher, or simply someone with a curious mind, our resources are here for you to use, remix, and learn from. Take them, make something wonderful, and share it — every project you create becomes a small resource others can learn from too. That is exactly the kind of open, generous learning we want to encourage.

We build what we promise

We don't just talk about efficiency and automation — we build it. This privacy-friendly tool is a live example of the technical standards we hold ourselves to: fast, clean, dependable, and made to last.
